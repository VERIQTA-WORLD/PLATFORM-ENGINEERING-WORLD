# Community conduct

Treat learners and contributors with respect. Critique ideas and evidence, not personal characteristics. Welcome questions, explain assumptions and make room for different levels of experience. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse and disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation. Use the hosting platform’s reporting tools for abuse or private-information exposure. Do not repeat private information in a public report. A technical disagreement is best resolved with a minimal reproduction, a primary reference or a clearly stated tradeoff.
