# Architecture decision record

## Title and status
A short decision, date and status: proposed, accepted, superseded or retired.

## Context
User task, constraints, scale, data sensitivity, operating capacity and assumptions.

## Decision
State the chosen boundary and behavior. Explain who owns it.

## Alternatives
Compare at least three plausible options, including retaining the current approach where relevant. Consider user impact, operations, security, cost and exit effort.

## Consequences
Benefits, new obligations, residual risks and unsupported scenarios.

## Validation
What evidence supports the decision? Which experiments remain unrun?

## Revisit triggers
Specify changes in workload, scale, risk or support cost that should reopen the decision. Include a migration or exit strategy.
