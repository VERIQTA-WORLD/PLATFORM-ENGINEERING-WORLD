# Learning evidence

## Scope
Lesson, date, tool versions and synthetic fixture names. Do not include private host paths or real organization identifiers in a public submission.

## Prediction
Expected result and the reasoning behind it.

## Procedure and result
Command or design procedure, exit status, relevant output and actual observation.

## Failure and repair
Controlled failure, diagnostic evidence, repair and final verification.

## Claim and limits
What this establishes, what remains unverified and the next experiment.
